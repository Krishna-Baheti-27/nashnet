# NashNet dataset

All files are generated by `node scripts/run-experiments.js --seeds 100` and are fully reproducible:
the same seed and configuration always give the same season. Seeds run from 1 to 100 in E1, E2, E3
and E5; E4 uses seed `100000 + 1000·g + m` for generation `g` and replicate `m`.

## Common columns (E1, E2, E5)

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `experiment` | E1, E2 or E5 |
| `ruleset` | `none`, `memory`, `rep`, `quota`, `pricing`, `audit`, `repaudit`, `all` |
| `supply` | `abundant` (M=14, r=0.7), `normal` (M=10, r=0.45), `shortage` (M=8, r=0.3) |
| `seed` | random seed of the season |
| `online` | tenants online at tick 150 (0–10) |
| `survival` | `online / 10` |
| `served` | jobs served / jobs demanded over the season |
| `cheat_adv` | mean credits of selfish team / mean credits of cooperative team − 1 |
| `coop_rate` | share of encounter actions that were Share |
| `gini` | Gini coefficient of final credits |
| `reserve` | mean zone fill at tick 150 (0–1) |
| `encounters` | number of encounters in the season |
| `pairs_met` | number of distinct tenant pairs that met at least once |
| `w_hat` | maximum-likelihood repeat-encounter probability, `1 − pairs_met / encounters` |
| `audits` | number of audit detections |
| `gifts` | number of donations (Share actions that transferred capacity) |
| `coop_offline` | cooperative tenants offline at tick 150 (of 7) |
| `selfish_offline` | selfish tenants offline at tick 150 (of 3) |
| `credits_<S>` | mean final credits of strategy S (TFT, ALLC, TRADER, REP, ALLD, AGGR) |

Extra columns: `audit_prob` (E2), `param` and `value` (E5: `noise` or `radius`).

## e3_timeseries.csv

Per-tick means over 100 shortage seasons for `none`, `rep`, `quota`, `audit`:
`tick`, `online`, `reserve`, `credits_coop_team`, `credits_selfish_team`, and `coop_rate_window`
(cooperation rate over the last 10 ticks).

## e4_replicator.csv

Discrete replicator dynamics, one row per (rule set, generation, strategy): `share` (population share
before reproduction), `count` (seats out of 10 after largest-remainder apportionment), `fitness` (mean
final credits of that strategy over 20 seasons).

## summary.json

Means and 95% t-confidence intervals for every condition, paired comparisons with Holm correction,
the audit sweep, analytic capacity quantities, and every other number reported in the README. Written by
`scripts/analysis/analyze.py`.
